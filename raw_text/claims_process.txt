Claims Submission and Processing Guide
1. Submit a Claim
Members should submit claims within 90 days of receiving medical services.
2. Required Information
Each claim must include:
- Member ID
- Plan ID
- Provider information
- Date of service
- Procedure
- Claim amount
3. Claim Status
Pending: The claim is still being reviewed.
Approved: The claim has been approved for payment.
Denied: The claim has been rejected.
4. Processing Time
Claims are normally processed within 14 business days.
5. Appeals
Members may appeal a denied claim within 30 days of receiving the denial notice.
